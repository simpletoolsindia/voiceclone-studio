# What makes Tamil animated story videos work

Research notes behind the story template ([STORY_PROMPT.md](STORY_PROMPT.md)) and the video layout. These come from reading channel stats, YouTube policy pages and creator retention guides (November 2026), not from watching videos. **[E]** means there is direct evidence and **[I]** means it is inferred.

## Pacing and structure

- **[E]** Show the conflict in the first 5–15 seconds. Slow logo or title intros lose viewers.
- **[E]** Something should happen every 20–30 seconds: a beat, joke, surprise or new problem. Peaks near 25%, 50% and 75% of the video help.
- **[E]** Typical average view percentage: 60–70% for videos under 5 minutes, 40–50% for 5–10 minutes.
- **[E]** Shorts: viewers decide in 1–2 seconds, and the picture should change every 2–3 seconds.
- **[I]** Scene title cards and "this scene is about…" lines stall the story. The studio no longer shows scene cards. Scene titles become YouTube chapter names.
- **[I]** What works for kids stories:
  - one clear want or problem
  - the rule of three (try, fail, try)
  - a catchphrase
  - a twist
  - a warm ending
  - one spoken lesson that the story itself shows
  - optionally, a tease of the next episode, so stories become a series with recurring characters

## Language

- **[I]** Use colloquial, simple spoken Tamil with light English words written in Tamil script, never textbook Tamil.
- **[I]** A warm grandma or grandpa storyteller voice is the classic narrator. The studio keeps the narrator brief and lets the characters carry the story through dialogue.

## Visuals

- **[E]** The leading new channels describe themselves as "3D animated stories" in a Pixar-like style.
- **[I]** Keep 2–4 consistent main characters with a signature colour or item. Use close-ups on emotional beats, slow push-ins, and big readable body movements.

## Titles and thumbnails

- **[E]** The common title pattern is: Tamil hook phrase | English translation | keyword stack. For example: "பேராசை பிரியாணி வியாபாரி | Greedy Biryani Seller | Tamil Moral Story".
- **[E]** Common keywords: Tamil Kathaigal, Tamil Moral Stories, Bedtime Stories Tamil, 3D Animated Stories.
- **[I]** For thumbnails, use one big emotional face, the key object, and 2–3 Tamil words.

## What to avoid (monetization)

- **[E]** YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (July 2025, clarified July 2026) can demonetize a whole channel for mass-produced, template-like videos with little variation between them, AI-made or not. Distressing "animal in danger, then rescued" content is also targeted.
- **[E]** The kids low-quality signals are: storylines that are hard to follow, keyword stuffing, bizarre or sensational content, and content that promotes bad behaviour.
- **[E]** Made for Kids videos lose personalized ads (roughly 50–80% lower revenue) as well as comments and end screens. Label your videos honestly anyway, because mislabeling carries COPPA/FTC risk.
- **[I]** To stay safe:
  - Make real creative choices for every video: your own story, your own edits through Fix-after-render.
  - Vary characters and settings.
  - Keep the AI disclosure on the end card.
